# Dataset, Video, and Model Asset Provenance & Licensing

This document details the source, provenance, and license for all datasets, video footage, synthetic assets, and models used across the Search-and-Rescue (SAR) Drone system.

## 2. Public Training Datasets

For on-device model training and fine-tuning, the following public academic and open datasets are supported:

### AIDER (Aerial Image Dataset for Emergency Response)
- **Creators:** K. Kyrkou and T. Theocharides (KIOS Research and Innovation Center of Excellence, University of Cyprus)
- **DOI / Link:** [10.5281/zenodo.3888300](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3888300)
- **Classes:** Fire/Smoke, Flood, Collapsed Building/Rubble, Traffic Collisions, Normal
- **License:** Open Access for Academic & Research Use
- **Used by:** `train_hazard_classifier.py` for whole-frame disaster hazard classification

### SARD (Search And Rescue Dataset)
- **Creators:** C2A Lab / Aerial Human SAR Community
- **Link:** [Zenodo / GitHub c2a-lab](https://github.com/c2a-lab)
- **Classes:** Humans (pedestrians, victims in forest, water, and debris environments from UAV nadir/oblique angles)
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0)
- **Used by:** `train_person_model.py` for YOLOv8 fine-tuning

### FLIR ADAS Thermal Dataset
- **Provider:** Teledyne FLIR LLC
- **Link:** [https://www.flir.com/oem/adas/adas-dataset-form/](https://www.flir.com/oem/adas/adas-dataset-form/)
- **Classes:** Thermal infrared (LWIR) imagery with annotations for People, Vehicles, and Bicycles
- **License:** Teledyne FLIR Open Dataset License for Non-Commercial / Research Use
- **Used by:** `train_thermal_model.py` for infrared hotspot detection

### c2a Aerial Human Dataset
- **Provider:** c2a-lab
- **Link:** [https://github.com/c2a-lab](https://github.com/c2a-lab)
- **Classes:** High and low altitude aerial survivor detection
- **License:** Academic & Research Open Use
- **Used by:** Combined with SARD in `train_person_model.py`
